# Hospital Emergency Room Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview

The Hospital Emergency Room Analysis project is an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze emergency room operations, patient flow, waiting times, admission patterns, patient satisfaction, and peak demand periods.

The objective is to transform raw healthcare data into actionable insights that can help healthcare administrators improve operational efficiency, optimize staff allocation, manage patient flow, and enhance the patient experience.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze total patient visits and emergency room activity.
- Monitor average patient waiting time against the 30-minute target.
- Evaluate patient admission and non-admission rates.
- Identify peak patient demand by day, time, and shift.
- Understand patient demographics and departmental referral patterns.
- Develop data-driven recommendations to improve emergency room operations.

## Tools and Technologies

- Power BI for interactive dashboards, KPI monitoring, and data visualization.
- Power Query for data cleaning, transformation, and preparation.
- DAX for measures and operational KPI calculations.
- Microsoft Excel for data inspection and preliminary analysis.

## Dataset Information

The dataset contains hospital emergency room visit records used to analyze patient demographics, arrival patterns, waiting times, admission status, patient satisfaction, and departmental referrals.

The dataset supports the evaluation of emergency room performance and helps identify trends in patient demand and operational efficiency.

## Data Cleaning and Preparation

Data cleaning and preparation were performed using Power Query in Power BI.

The main steps included:

- Checking data quality and consistency.
- Removing duplicate records where applicable.
- Handling missing and inconsistent values.
- Correcting data types and formatting.
- Preparing date and time fields for trend analysis.
- Creating calculated columns and measures required for reporting.

## Key Performance Indicators

- Total Patient Visits
- Average Patient Waiting Time
- Patient Satisfaction Score
- Patient Admission Rate
- Patient Non-Admission Rate
- Monthly Patient Visits
- Patient Visits by Day and Time
- Patient Demographic Distribution
- Departmental Referral Analysis

## Key Takeaways and Business Insights

The dashboard provides an overview of emergency room activity and helps identify patterns in patient arrivals, waiting times, admission outcomes, and patient demographics.

Monthly analysis helps track changes in patient volume over time, while demographic and referral analysis provides additional context for understanding patient demand.

The dashboard also supports the monitoring of waiting times against the 30-minute target and helps identify areas that may require further investigation.
